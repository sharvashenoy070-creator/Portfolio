Portfolio · About me

Meet Sharva Shenoy: engineer, leader, creator

B.Tech IT student at NMIMS Shirpur. Drones and AI, campus leadership, marketing and stories. Open to internships, collaborations and event partnerships.

FIG. 1 — The Operations Lead, addressing the room. Artist’s impression; a proper photograph is on its way.

SHIRPUR — At NMIMS, where the B.Tech in Information Technology is only the first line of the timetable, Sharva Shenoy has become the name organisers call when a festival needs to start on time. He is Vice President of Avinya, leads event management and documentation for Google Developer Groups (GDG) on Campus and event management for the Coding Club, and has sat on the organising committees of Ambiora and Protsahan, and is a former member of ADC and RAW Vision Media.

This year he also represents Mood Indigo ’26 of IIT Bombay on campus, and at Protsahan ’26 he cracked a deal with Domino’s Pizza that brought a three-day stall to the campus fest. Away from the stage he has worked as a paid marketing intern at Corizo and supervised plant operations and inventory at Solarika Technocrats in Nashik. He speaks English, Hindi and Marathi, and lists marketing, technology, AI tools, drone technology and leadership among his professional interests.

The Engineer: drones, AI and a steady hand

FIG. 2 — A drone surveys the festival grounds.

Whether the topic is drone technology, robotics, AI tools or the IT lab, Shenoy’s curiosity runs towards systems that sense, move and think. In a one-time stint with SwarmHack he coordinated internal Smart India Hackathon preparation, a mission now complete, and he coordinates technical events for Techfiesta ’27.

The Leader: the show goes on, on time

FIG. 3 — Opening night, as seen from the control desk.

Teams, logistics, sponsors and stages are his daily weather. As Vice President of Avinya, EM and Documentation Lead at GDG on Campus and event-management head at the Coding Club, he plans, delegates and keeps everyone on the same channel until the last light goes out.

The Creator: words, design and brand

FIG. 4 — Ideas are drafted before they are polished.

He writes for The Writer’s Hub, promotes campaigns through his marketing internship at Corizo, and is a former member of RAW Vision Media (graphic design) and ADC (marketing). Every idea is tested like a prototype, with an entrepreneur’s curiosity.

Plant supervisor keeps inventory, crews and sunshine in order
